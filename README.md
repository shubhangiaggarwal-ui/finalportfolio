## 🎨 UI/UX Design System & Aesthetics

This portfolio was designed adhering to modern high-end editorial principles inspired by contemporary design studios and award-winning minimalist engineering showcases:

- **Color Palette (Muted Tones)**:
  - **Navy Blue (`#0B1325`, `#1A243B`)**: Represents analytical depth, architectural stability, and engineering rigor.
  - **Warm Sand Beige (`#FBF8F3`, `#F5EFEB`, `#EFE8DC`)**: Organic, clean paper canvas background that eliminates sterile bright white glare.
  - **Regal Burgundy (`#6E1B2D`, `#8C253B`, `#F4E8EA`)**: Creative accent color signifying artistic curiosity, refined passion, and human touch.
  - **Jet Black (`#111215`)**: Precise, sharp typographic contrast.
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - **Display / Titles**: `Syne` (Bold, chic, modern geometric sans-serif)
  - **Body / Interface**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Ultra-crisp, highly readable grotesque)
  - **Editorial Flourish**: `Playfair Display` (Italicized warmth for creative phrases)
  - **Monospace**: `JetBrains Mono` (Terminal snippets & credential tags)
- **Visuals & Artistry**:
  - Interactive **Generative Abstract Canvas** in pastel gradients that react subtly with ambient motion.
  - Modern abstract composition cards with 3D-styled floating chips and badges.
  - Subtle noise grain overlay providing warmth and depth.
